Delete your Dailance account

Proposed revision: 14 November 2026

Effective date: To be set when this revision is approved and published

Publication approval required. These proposed instructions reflect the implemented deletion flow but are not effective until approved and published.

Dailance provides account deletion directly inside the mobile application. There is no web deletion form.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Dailance.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the warning and select Continue.
  5. Confirm that the request is yours:
    • For an email-and-password account, enter your current password.
    • For an Apple-only account without a local password, select Confirm with Apple and delete and complete the fresh Apple authorization.
  6. Submit the irreversible deletion request.

What happens next

For an Apple-only account, Dailance revokes its stored Apple authorization before durably accepting the deletion request. After Dailance accepts either type of request, you are signed out, active refresh sessions and password-recovery state are disabled, and the account cannot be used while deletion completes asynchronously. The app does not show a progress tracker, and no exact processing time is promised.

Completion removes user-owned account and authentication records, profile data, plans, income and essential-expense items, savings settings, expense transactions, custom categories and preferences, daily-budget projections, analytics, and pending user-linked email and push-delivery records from active Dailance databases. Global system categories and operational records that do not identify your account are not user-owned data and are not removed.

When the deletion process has completed, Dailance sends a confirmation email to the account email address. A delay or failure in delivering that email does not undo the completed deletion.

A limited cryptographic security marker remains to prevent deleted data from being recreated or stale requests from being accepted. It does not contain your email address, raw account identifier, credentials, or financial records. Some copies may remain in encrypted backups until those backups expire or are replaced. Limited security, infrastructure, or email-provider records may also remain where reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or operational purposes. Final backup, log, and provider retention schedules still require confirmation. For details, read the Privacy Policy.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot access the app or your account, contact support@dailance.com. You may use that address to request deletion assistance when the in-app method is unavailable. An email address alone is not sufficient to authorize deletion; support must use an approved secure process to verify account control before a request is accepted.

Do not send your password, verification code, recovery code, or access token by email. Support may need to verify that you control the account before assisting.
