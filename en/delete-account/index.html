Account help

Delete your Dailance account

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Dailance provides account deletion directly inside the mobile application. There is no web deletion form.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Dailance.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the warning.
  5. Complete the confirmation steps.
  6. Submit the deletion request.

What happens next

Account deletion is intended to remove your Dailance account and associated personal financial data. Processing may be asynchronous inside Dailance, so the app may temporarily show the account as being deleted. After deletion is finalized, access to the account will no longer be available.

No exact processing time is promised. For more information about data handling and possible limited retention for legal, security, operational, or backup purposes, read the Privacy Policy.

If you cannot access your account

If you cannot access your account or need help with deletion, contact support@dailance.com.

Do not send your password, verification code, recovery code, or access token by email. Support may need to verify that you control the account before assisting.
