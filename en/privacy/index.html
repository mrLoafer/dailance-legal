1. Who operates Dailance

Dailance is the product and service name of a personal budgeting application operated by an individual (the “operator,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). Dailance is not the operator's legal identity.

The operator's verified legal name must replace the review field in section 12 before publication. The initial public release is for adults aged 18 or older and is intended for distribution through the Armenia storefronts. Dailance does not currently claim geographical access controls.

2. Information we process

  • Account data: email address, password hash, verification and recovery state, authentication sessions, and internal identifiers. If you use Sign in with Apple, we also process the Apple account identifier, the email Apple shares with us (which may be a private relay address), and an encrypted revocation credential.
  • Profile data: optional display name, currency preference, timezone, language, and reminder preferences.
  • Budgeting data: monthly plans, income and essential-expense names and amounts, savings settings, expenses, dates, currencies, categories, daily-budget calculations, carry-over, and analytics derived from information you enter.
  • Technical data: request and correlation identifiers, timestamps, response status, session, device, network, security, delivery, and diagnostic information. Network infrastructure may process IP addresses.
  • Notification data: email delivery data and, if you enable reminders, an encrypted Expo push token and bounded delivery state. Reminder messages are generic and do not contain balances or expense amounts.

Dailance does not connect to bank accounts, import bank transactions, collect payment-card or purchase information, sell personal information, show advertising, or use an advertising or behavioral-analytics SDK.

3. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts; provide budgeting, expense, reminder, and analytics features; send transactional messages; respond to support and privacy requests; prevent misuse; diagnose failures; maintain backups; comply with applicable law; and process account deletion.

4. Service providers

The Dailance backend is hosted on DigitalOcean infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany. Encrypted database backups are stored in a private DigitalOcean Spaces bucket. Healthchecks.io receives a generic backup-job heartbeat for infrastructure monitoring, not backup contents or user financial data.

Production transactional email is designed to use Resend for verification, recovery, and deletion-completion messages; the production account and sender configuration must be verified before publication. Messages sent to support@dailance.com pass through Cloudflare Email Routing to a private operator-controlled mailbox.

If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple processes authentication and revocation requests. If you enable iOS push reminders, Expo and the Apple Push Notification service process the device routing token and generic reminder content. GitHub Pages hosts this tracker-free legal site and may process ordinary web-request metadata.

These providers do not operate Dailance. Their current terms, account settings, processing locations, retention, deletion controls, and any required international-transfer safeguards must be verified before publication. This policy does not claim a certification or an unverified processor-protection guarantee.

5. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers described above as needed to operate Dailance, when required by valid legal process, to protect users or the service, or as part of a future transfer to another operator with any notice and protections required by law. No operator transition has occurred. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

6. Retention and account deletion

While your account is active, we retain account and budgeting information as needed to provide and secure Dailance. You can request deletion from Profile → Delete account. After the request is accepted, the app signs you out, session refresh and password recovery are disabled, and deletion continues in the background across Dailance services. No exact completion time is promised. We send a confirmation email after active-system deletion completes; an email delay or failure does not undo deletion.

Completion removes the account, profile, plans, expenses, categories and preferences, daily-budget projections, analytics, and pending user-linked notification records from active application databases. Limited keyed cryptographic markers remain to prevent stale data or requests from recreating a deleted account. They do not contain the email address, raw account identifier, credentials, or financial records.

Historical encrypted backups are not selectively rewritten when an account is deleted. The approved backup policy selects the newest valid restore point on each of 14 represented UTC dates, the newest valid point in each of 8 represented ISO weeks, and always the latest valid point. This count-based selection is not a fixed maximum age: cleanup can be delayed or fail, the latest valid point is preserved, and provider-held object versions or snapshots may follow separate settings. Automatic retention deployment and provider-version settings must be verified before publication. A historical restore must remain isolated unless newer deletion protections are preserved and reapplied so deleted accounts cannot return as active accounts.

Limited logs and provider records may remain where needed for security, service operation, legal obligations, or disputes. Final log periods, provider retention, and any mandatory legal retention remain to be confirmed; we do not promise an unsupported deadline.

7. Security

We use safeguards including password hashing, access controls, encrypted network connections, encrypted backups, service separation, and security monitoring. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

8. International processing

Your information is processed on the Dailance backend in Germany and may be processed in other countries where the providers above operate. Required transfer safeguards must be confirmed from the actual production arrangements before publication.

9. Your choices and rights

You can update supported profile and budgeting information, manage reminders, delete supported expenses, and request account deletion in the app. Applicable Armenian law may also provide rights to information, access, correction, blocking, destruction, and complaint, subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

For a privacy request, email support@dailance.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to confirm account control. Do not email your password, verification code, recovery code, access token, identity documents, or complete financial records.

10. Adults only

Dailance is for users aged 18 or older and is not intended for children or minors under 18. Contact us if you believe a minor has provided information contrary to this requirement.
