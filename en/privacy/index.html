1. Introduction

Dailance is the product and service name of a personal budgeting application; it is not the name of a company or other legal entity. The service is currently provided by a natural person acting in an individual capacity (the “operator,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). No legal entity or registered sole proprietorship currently operates Dailance.

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use Dailance. The operator's verified legal name must replace the review field in section 21 before this revision is published.

The initial public release is intended for distribution only through the Armenia storefronts of the Apple App Store and Google Play. Storefront selection is not a technical guarantee that the service blocks access from every other location, and the backend does not currently claim geographical access controls.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide, information created as you use budgeting features, and limited technical and security information needed to operate the service. Dailance does not currently connect to bank accounts, import bank transactions, or collect receipt photos.

Dailance is completely free at its initial public release and has no paid subscriptions, in-app purchases, paid features, advertisements, or other monetization. We therefore do not collect payment-card or purchase information for Dailance. If monetization is proposed in the future, we will review and update this policy and provide clear notice before any related processing begins, as required by applicable law.

3. Account information

For an email-and-password account, we collect your email address and information about account status, email verification, password recovery, authentication sessions, and internal account and session identifiers. Your password is stored by our systems as a secure password hash, not as readable plaintext.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple performs the authentication. We process the verified Apple account identifier, the email address Apple shares with us (which may be an Apple private relay address), whether Apple identifies the email as private, and an encrypted Apple credential used to revoke Dailance's authorization when required. This Apple-related processing occurs only when you choose Sign in with Apple and the feature is enabled. We do not automatically combine an Apple account with an existing password account merely because their email addresses match.

4. Profile information

We may collect an optional profile name, your chosen currency preference, and your timezone. Timezone information is used to apply dates and monthly periods consistently.

5. Financial plan information

We collect the monthly plan period and currency, planned income names and amounts, planned essential expense names and amounts, and your selected savings percentage. We also create budget values derived from the information you enter, such as planned savings, available monthly budget, daily budget, and carry-over.

6. Expense information

We collect expenses you enter manually, including expense names, amounts, currencies, dates, and related plan and category information. We use this information to maintain expense history, calculate daily balances, and provide basic analytics.

7. Category information

We process the system categories you choose and custom category names you create, rename, archive, or associate with expenses. Category information may remain in historical expense records so those records retain their meaning.

8. Technical and security information

We process app, session, request, device, network, and security metadata reasonably needed to authenticate users, maintain sessions, prevent misuse, diagnose failures, deliver the service, and protect its infrastructure. This may include internal identifiers, request and correlation identifiers, timestamps, IP addresses processed by network infrastructure, response status, and security event information. We do not intentionally collect passwords, verification codes, recovery codes, or access tokens in application logs.

9. Email and notification data

We process your email address, message type, delivery status, and limited operational metadata to send email-verification, password-recovery, account-deletion completion, security, and other transactional messages. Email content may include a short-lived verification or recovery code where needed.

If you opt in to push reminders and the feature is enabled, we process your daily-expense and monthly-budget reminder preferences, selected daily reminder time, timezone-derived schedule, language, installation and session identifiers, encrypted Expo push token, token fingerprint, and bounded delivery and receipt state. Both reminder types start disabled. Reminder messages are generic and do not include balances, expense amounts, your email address, or your Dailance account identifier. You can turn reminders off in Dailance and control notification permission in your device settings.

10. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create, verify, authenticate, and secure accounts;
  • provide profiles, monthly plans, expenses, categories, daily budgets, carry-over, and analytics;
  • send essential account and service messages;
  • respond to support and privacy requests;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, and improve service reliability; and
  • process account deletion.

11. Legal, security, and operational purposes

We may use information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, enforce our terms, detect or prevent fraud and abuse, investigate security incidents, protect users and the service, establish or defend legal claims, and maintain necessary business records.

12. Service providers

The companies and platforms listed in this section are external service providers. They process limited information to provide infrastructure, communications, authentication, or delivery services; they do not operate Dailance and are not the legal person offering the service. Their precise controller, processor, independent-controller, or equivalent role must be confirmed from the actual production arrangements before publication.

We use Resend to deliver transactional emails such as account verification, password recovery, and account-deletion completion messages. Resend may process the recipient email address, message content, short-lived verification or recovery codes where applicable, delivery status, and related operational metadata for that purpose.

Messages sent to support@dailance.com are received through Cloudflare Email Routing and privately forwarded to an operator-controlled mailbox. Cloudflare and the destination mailbox provider may process the sender and recipient addresses, message subject and content, attachments, routing information, and delivery metadata. The destination mailbox provider, account settings, processing locations, retention, and deletion controls still require operator confirmation.

The Dailance backend is hosted on DigitalOcean infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany. DigitalOcean may process account, financial, technical, and network-request data sent to the service. The exact DigitalOcean contracting entity, accepted processing terms, subprocessors, provider-side retention, and account-level controls still require operator confirmation.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple processes authorization and credential-revocation requests. If you opt in to push reminders and remote delivery is enabled, Expo and Apple Push Notification service process device routing tokens and generic reminder content for the iOS application. Firebase Cloud Messaging would also process that information only if Android remote delivery is enabled. These providers do not receive Dailance account or financial data from us for these features beyond the data described here.

The public legal website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-request and network metadata when it delivers these pages, but the legal website does not receive Dailance account or financial data and does not use tracking scripts or cookies.

Encrypted database backups are stored in a private DigitalOcean Spaces bucket. Backup archives are encrypted before upload. The Spaces storage region, account-level access controls, key custody, accepted processing terms, provider-side retention and deletion behavior still require operator confirmation before public release.

13. Data sharing

We may share information with service providers as described above; when required by law or valid legal process; when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security; or as part of a future transfer of the service to another operator, subject to appropriate protections and legally required notice. No transfer to a company or other operator has occurred. Dailance does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with advertisers.

14. Data retention

While an account is active, we retain its account, profile, financial, session, preference, and notification records for as long as needed to provide and secure Dailance. Different information may be kept for different periods. A final application-log retention period has not been approved, and provider-side retention must be confirmed before publication.

When an account deletion request is completed, account and financial data associated with the account is removed from active application databases. A limited cryptographic security marker that does not contain the account email, raw account identifier, or financial records remains to prevent deleted data from being recreated or stale requests from being accepted. Existing encrypted backups are not selectively rewritten when an account is deleted. Automatic deletion of old backup restore points is not currently enabled, so residual copies may remain until the operator securely deletes the affected restore points or activates an approved rotation schedule. A restore must preserve or reapply deletion protections before restored data is returned to service.

Limited security or infrastructure logs and transactional-email provider records may remain temporarily or for longer where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, disputes, or service operation. Dailance does not currently promise a fixed deletion period for backups, logs, or provider records because the backup rotation, log schedule, provider settings, and any legally required retention have not been approved. We may also retain information when applicable law requires it.

15. Account deletion

You can request deletion in the Dailance application from Profile → Delete account. An email-and-password account must confirm with its current password. An Apple-only account without a local password must complete a fresh, user-bound Apple authorization. For an Apple-only account, Dailance revokes its stored Apple authorization before durably accepting the deletion request.

After the request is accepted, you are signed out, active refresh sessions are revoked, recovery state is cleared, and processing continues asynchronously across Dailance services. Completion removes the account and its associated profile, plans, income and essential-expense items, savings settings, expenses, category preferences, daily-budget projections, analytics, and pending user-linked email or push-delivery records from active application databases, subject to the limited retention described above.

When deletion has completed across Dailance services, we send a confirmation email to the account email address. Email delivery is a separate notification step, so an email-provider delay or failure does not undo the completed deletion.

See Delete Account for step-by-step instructions. If you cannot access your account, contact support@dailance.com.

16. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. These include password hashing, access controls, encrypted network connections, service separation, and security monitoring appropriate to the service. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your information is processed on the Dailance backend in Germany and may also be processed in other countries where the operator or service providers listed above operate. Privacy laws in those countries may differ. The exact processing locations for email, authentication, notification, legal-site, and backup services, and any required international-transfer safeguards, have not all been confirmed and must be approved before public release. We will not claim a particular transfer mechanism unless it is actually in place.

18. Children

Dailance is not intended for children or minors under 18. You must be at least 18 years old to create or use an account. If you believe a person under 18 has provided information contrary to these requirements, contact us so we can review it and take appropriate action.

19. Your choices and rights

You can update supported profile and financial information, edit or delete expenses where the app provides those controls, and request account deletion in the app. Armenian law may provide rights to information about processing and to request access, correction, blocking, or destruction in applicable circumstances, as well as a right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Agency or a court. The scope and procedure may be subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

To make a privacy request, email support@dailance.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to confirm your identity and control of the relevant account. Do not send your password, verification code, recovery code, or access token by email. Account deletion inside the app remains the primary deletion method when you can access your account.
